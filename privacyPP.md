# Player Passport – Privacy Notice

## Who we are

Player Passport is a skills development tracker app that sports clubs use to monitor player ability within different benchmarked attributes. It is run by Lucy Lloyd ("we", "us") and is available at app.progresssquad.co.uk. In this notice, "club" means any club, or district, county or national youth pathway programme, that uses Player Passport.

Your club decides which players and coaches are added and what is recorded about them. In data protection terms, the club is the **controller** of that information and we are its **processor**: we store and handle it to run the app for the club. With the club's agreement, we also use it to create anonymised, combined statistics that never identify a player (see "Anonymised insights" below) but can give us insights into effective coaching and sports development. We never use personal information for anything else.

This notice explains what information the app holds, who can see it and what your rights are. It is written for players, parents and carers, coaches and club officials.

## What information we hold

We hold only what is needed to record player development.

| Whose information | What we hold |
| --- | --- |
| Players | First and last name; the squad they belong to (such as under14s); scores against the club's chosen attributes; written feedback from coaches on any context on the attribute scoring |
| Coaches and club admins | Email address (used to log in); which squads they are allocated to |

We do not collect players' dates of birth, addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, photos or health information.

## Why we use it

Player information is used so coaches can track each player's progress over time, give them useful feedback and shape training sessions around their development. The lawful basis is the club's **legitimate interests** in coaching and developing its players, which players and parents would reasonably expect from a club they have joined.

Coach email addresses are used only to let coaches log in securely and to send them login links.

We never sell personal information or use it for advertising, and we never share it with anyone outside the club except the service providers listed below. Anonymised statistics are different, as explained in the next section.

## Anonymised insights

With each club's agreement, we combine scores from many players, across squads and clubs, into anonymised statistics. Examples include typical score ranges for a skill in an age group, or patterns in how training performance relates to match performance. These help coaches understand what good progress looks like and will allow us to build intelligence on the best benchmarked attributes.

- **No names or feedback text.** Statistics are built from scores, measurements such as timings, attributes and broad age bands. Names and the text of written feedback are never included, though we may draw general themes (such as "first touch" or "positioning") and measurements from feedback in a form that cannot identify anyone. Measurements are rounded or grouped into bands so that unusual results cannot single out a player.
- **Large groups only.** We never produce a figure from fewer than 10 players, so no individual can be picked out.
- **What we may do with them.** Once statistics are anonymised they no longer relate to any player, so they are not personal information. We may use or share them, including commercially, for example by licensing benchmarks or coaching insights to other organisations. We never sell or share any player's individual records.
- **If a record is deleted.** Deleting a player's record removes all of their personal information. Statistics already created from many players are not affected, because nothing in them relates to that player.

Creating these statistics relies on our own **legitimate interests** in improving coaching insight. Because many players are children, we leave out names and feedback text and only use large groups. You can object at any time by contacting your club or us, and we will leave that player's information out of future statistics.

## Who can see it

Access is controlled in the database itself, not just in the app's screens. A coach can only see the squads they have been allocated to. A club can also appoint a lead, such as a director of sport, who can see all of that club's squads. Nobody can see another club's information. If a player is with both a club and a pathway programme, each keeps its own separate record, and neither can see the other's.

| Who | What they can see |
| --- | --- |
| A squad's allocated coach | Players, scores and feedback for that squad only |
| A club lead (e.g. director of sport), if the club appoints one | Players, scores and feedback for every squad at their own club, but no other club |
| Other coaches at the club | Nothing about squads they are not allocated to |
| The app administrator (Lucy Lloyd) | All records, for setting up clubs and squads, support and fixing problems only |

Players and parents do not log in to the app. If you want to see what is held about you or your child, see "Your rights" below.

## Service providers and where data is stored

We use a small number of providers to run the app. Each handles data only on our instructions.

| Provider | What it does | Data it handles |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Supabase | Database and login system | All player records and coach email addresses, stored in Frankfurt, Germany (EU) |
| Resend | Sends login links to coaches | Coach email addresses |
| GitHub Pages | Hosts the app's web pages | No player data; logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes |

All player records are stored in the EU, which UK law recognises as giving the same level of protection as the UK. Supabase is a US company. Where any of these providers may access data from outside the UK, the transfer is covered by their data processing terms, which include the UK's approved safeguards for international transfers.

## Retention period

A player's record stays with them for as long as they play at the club, so coaches can follow their development as they move up through age groups, for example from Under 12s to Under 14s and on into a senior squad. Their squad might change each season, and earlier scores and feedback stay part of their development history. If a club changes the attributes it scores, earlier scores are kept under the attributes used at the time.

Clubs review their squads at the start of each season and remove players who have left from their coaches access.

| Information | How long we keep it |
| --- | --- |
| A player's development record (name, current and past squads, scores, feedback) | While the player is at the club. Deleted within 12 months of them leaving, or sooner if the club removes them |
| Squad summaries (average scores per attribute for a squad in a season, with no names or feedback) | While the club uses Player Passport, so it can compare squads year on year. Only created for squads of 10 or more players |
| A coach's account and email address | Until the club removes the coach |
| Everything held for a club | Deleted within 90 days of the club stopping using Player Passport, unless the club asks for a copy first |

Deleted information may remain in Supabase's automatic backups for a short period before those backups expire.

## Children's information

Many players are under 18, so we keep what is recorded about them to a minimum: a name, a squad, scores and coaching feedback. Children do not use the app themselves.

Coaches are asked to write feedback as they would say it to the player and their parents, and not to record health, injury, family or safeguarding information in the app. Any concerns of that kind should go through the club's welfare officer instead.

Parents and carers can ask about their child's information on the child's behalf. Older children can usually make requests themselves.

## Keeping it secure

- All connections to the app use HTTPS encryption.
- Coaches log in with a one-time link sent to their email, so there are no passwords to steal or reuse.
- Database rules ensure each coach can only reach the squads they are allocated to, and each club lead only their own club.
- Administrator access is limited to Lucy Lloyd.
- The app uses only the browser storage it needs to keep you logged in. It has no advertising or tracking cookies.

If we ever discover a security breach affecting your club's data, we will tell the club promptly so it can decide what to do next.

## Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

- get a copy of the information held about you or your child;
- have inaccurate information corrected;
- ask for information to be deleted;
- object to, or ask us to limit, how it is used.

The quickest route is usually to ask your club, as it controls the records. You can also contact us directly and we will work with the club to respond, normally within one month.

If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at [ico.org.uk](https://ico.org.uk) or on 0303 123 1113. We would appreciate the chance to put things right first.
